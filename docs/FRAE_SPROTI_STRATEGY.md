# Tækniþróunarsjóður: Fræ to Sproti Strategy

## Current Positioning

Ratatoskur is an early-stage edtech product prototype for AI-assisted feedback on handwritten mathematics work.

The near-term plan is to apply for **Tækniþróunarsjóður Fræ** as a preparation and validation grant, then use the results to support a later **Sproti** application when that call opens.

## Confirmation From Tækniþróunarsjóður

On 2 June 2026, Tækniþróunarsjóður confirmed by email that receiving a Fræ grant does **not** negatively affect a later Sproti application.

Their guidance:

- Fræ can be used to shape and prepare a project before applying for a larger development grant.
- A later Sproti application must clearly show the next development step.
- The Sproti work should build on the Fræ work.
- The same costs or same work packages should not be funded twice.

## Fræ Framing

Fræ should be framed as validation and preparation work:

- technical feasibility of robust handwritten math feedback
- structured user testing with students and teachers
- market and business model validation
- privacy, consent, and data-use design
- evaluation methodology for AI tutoring quality
- product roadmap and Sproti-ready development plan

## Sproti Framing Later

Sproti should be framed as the next larger development step:

- production-grade app/backend development
- broader pilot deployments
- teacher-facing dashboard
- stronger model evaluation and routing infrastructure
- commercialization and go-to-market readiness

## Important Boundary

Do not request Sproti funding for the same costs or work packages funded by Fræ.

Suggested separation:

- **Fræ:** validation, feasibility, prototype testing, business planning.
- **Sproti:** scaled development, deployment hardening, pilot expansion, productization.
