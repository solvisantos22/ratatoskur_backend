# Ratatoskur Product Brief

Ratatoskur is an AI math coach for handwritten student work. It gives students a notebook workflow where they can upload a problem, write a solution, and receive feedback in Icelandic without losing ownership of the solving process.

## Problem

Students often need feedback while solving mathematics, not only after submitting a final answer. Existing AI tools can solve problems, but they often skip the student's process or reveal too much too early. Handwritten work adds another challenge: the system must deal with ambiguous symbols, partial reasoning, and multi-page solutions.

## Product Hypothesis

Students and teachers will benefit from an AI tutor that:

- reads both the problem image and the handwritten solution
- gives mode-specific feedback: hint, solution check, reveal
- asks for clarification when handwriting is uncertain
- stores attempts and feedback for learning analytics
- supports Icelandic math learning contexts

## Current Stage

Ratatoskur is an early-stage product prototype. The near-term focus is validation: technical feasibility, student/teacher user testing, business model exploration, privacy and consent design, and preparation for a larger Sproti-stage development project.

## Primary Users

- Students in upper-secondary and early university mathematics.
- Teachers and tutors who want better visibility into student reasoning and recurring errors.
- Researchers interested in Icelandic educational AI and AI-supported math learning.
